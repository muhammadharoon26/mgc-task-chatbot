# MGC Aurora Heights — Project Brochure

*MGC Developments · Mian Group of Chakwal · Issued March 2025*

## Overview

MGC Aurora Heights is a 22-storey mixed-use residential tower on GT Road, Islamabad,
approximately 4 km from Al-Jannat Mall. The project sits on 3.8 acres and comprises
418 residential units across two connected blocks (Block A and Block B), with a
commercial podium occupying floors 1 to 3.

Groundbreaking took place in November 2024. Structural completion of Block A is
scheduled for Q1 2027. Block B follows two quarters later. Possession for Block A
units is targeted for **Q3 2027**.

All units above floor 12 in Block B face the Margalla range. Studio and 1-Bed units
are not available with a Margalla-facing orientation.

## Amenities

- Rooftop infinity pool and residents' lounge (floor 22, Block A)
- Two-level basement parking, 640 slots
- 100% backup power via three 1,250 kVA generators
- Gymnasium, prayer area, and 4,000 sq ft community hall on the podium level
- 24/7 security with facial-recognition access at both tower entrances
- Fibre-to-the-unit internet, pre-wired for two providers
- Central firefighting system compliant with CDA fire safety bylaws 2019

## Commercial Podium

Floors 1 through 3 host 74 commercial units ranging from 280 sq ft to 2,100 sq ft.
Ground floor is reserved for food and beverage tenants. Anchor tenancy discussions
are ongoing; no anchor tenant has been confirmed as of this brochure's issue date.

## Approvals

The project is approved by the Capital Development Authority under NOC reference
CDA/PLW/NOC-2024/1187, dated 12 August 2024. The layout plan was approved on
3 October 2024. Environmental clearance from Pak-EPA was granted in September 2024.
