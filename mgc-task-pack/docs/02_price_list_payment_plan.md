## Location Premiums

Applied on top of base price:

- Floors 13–19: **+4%**
- Floors 20–22: **+7%**
- Corner unit: **+3%**
- Margalla-facing: **+6%**

Premiums are cumulative. A Margalla-facing corner unit on floor 15 carries
+4% +3% +6% = +13% over base.

## Standard Payment Plan (4 years)

| Milestone | Share | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Booking / token | 10% | Non-refundable after 30 days |
| Confirmation | 10% | Due within 45 days of booking |
| 16 quarterly instalments | 60% | 3.75% each |
| On possession | 20% | Due at handover |

## Discounts

- **Full cash payment:** 12% discount on base price. Location premiums are not discounted.
- **50% upfront:** 6% discount on base price.
- **Overseas Pakistani (Roshan Digital Account):** additional 2%, stackable with the above.
- **Second unit by the same buyer:** 1.5% on the second unit only.

Discounts do not apply to commercial podium units.

## Other Charges

- Utility connection and meter charges: 285,000 per residential unit
- Parking slot (mandatory, 1 per unit): 1,450,000
- Additional parking slot: 1,650,000
- Maintenance advance: 12 months at 9.50 per sq ft, payable at possession
- Transfer fee (before possession): 2% of the current list price

## Notes

Prices are subject to revision. Bookings made before 30 June 2025 are protected
against the next revision. A booking is confirmed only once the token amount clears
into the MGC Developments escrow account maintained at the project's designated bank.

Payment plans for commercial podium units are issued separately on request.
