# MGC Developments — Booking Policy & Sales FAQ

*Internal sales reference. Version 2.1, updated May 2025.*

## Booking Process

1. Buyer selects a unit and submits Form MGC-B1 with CNIC copy (or passport for overseas buyers).
2. Token amount is paid into the project escrow account. Cash is not accepted above PKR 500,000.
3. Allocation letter is issued within 7 working days of the token clearing.
4. Confirmation payment falls due within 45 days.
5. On receipt of confirmation payment, the buyer signs the Sale Agreement.

## Cancellations and Refunds

If a buyer cancels within 30 days of booking, the token is refundable less a
processing deduction of PKR 75,000. After 30 days, the token is non-refundable.

If a buyer defaults on three consecutive quarterly instalments, MGC may cancel the
allocation. In that case the buyer is refunded all amounts paid, less 15% of the
total unit price as liquidated damages, paid out within 180 days.

## Transfers

Units may be transferred to a third party after the confirmation payment has been
made and at least four quarterly instalments have cleared. Transfer fee is **2.5%
of the current list price**. Both parties must attend the head office in person;
transfers by power of attorney require prior written approval from MGC management.

## Overseas Buyers

Overseas Pakistanis may book remotely. Payments must originate from a Roshan Digital
Account or a declared foreign remittance channel. Overseas buyers receive an
additional 2% discount and are exempt from the in-person requirement at the transfer
stage, provided documents are attested by the relevant Pakistani mission.

## Possession

Possession is offered once the completion certificate is issued and all dues are
cleared. Buyers receive 30 days' notice. A holding charge of PKR 25,000 per month
applies if the buyer does not take possession within 60 days of the notice.

## Frequently Asked

**Is a home loan available?**
MGC has arrangements in principle with two banks. Terms, eligibility and mark-up
rates are set by the bank, not by MGC. Sales staff must not quote mark-up rates.

**Can the booked unit be changed to a different unit?**
One change is permitted before the fourth instalment, subject to availability and
payment of any price difference. No change is permitted after that.

**What is the rental yield?**
MGC does not publish rental yield projections and sales staff must not give
projections verbally. Direct such queries to the marketing manager.

**Is the project gas-connected?**
Sui gas connection has been applied for. No confirmed timeline is available.
